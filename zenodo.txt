Gravitational anomalies across astrophysical scales exhibit a dual scaling regularity: the radius at which Newtonian dynamics fails scales as R_T(M) = (3M/4pi rho_T)^(1/3) propto M^(1/3) in the compact saturation regime and R_DM propto M^(1/2) in the low-acceleration galactic regime. Within the Temporal Equivalence Principle (TEP) two-branch scalar action (P(X,phi) = P_kin(X) - (1/4) lambda M_Pl^4 u^4, A(u) = e^(-u)), the quartic confining potential fixes the cubic screening response S = (rho/rho_T)^(1/3) around the saturation density rho_T ~ 20 g/cm^3 (calibrated from terrestrial GNSS and tested across astrophysical systems), while the nonlinear kinetic branch P_,X(xi) = k sqrt(xi) + 2xi (k = 16.03) governs unscreened (S ≪ 1) galactic halos at a_eff = 1.20e-10 m s^-2 (predicting a star-forming spiral BBH host tracking fraction ⟨ε_host⟩_SFR^UCD = 0.236 +- 0.054 [0.197 with 25% bulge/NSC hosts] that matches the GWTC-1-5.0 optimum in Paper 22). Across a 4-layer PREM Earth boundary-value problem and 1-D spherical nonlinear Picard solve, the transition-shell (4,010 km; 3,957 km nonlinear) and full-volume (3,213 km; 3,170 km nonlinear, L_c^vol/R_T = 0.775) kernels reproduce both the 25.3-year GPS PPP clock correlation length (L_c = 4,201 km, rho_T = 19.2 g/cm^3) and 2023 CDDIS RINEX3 single-constellation GPS, Galileo, and GLONASS reprocessing (3,853 km sqrt(N)-weighted, and 3,155 +- 181 km [CV = 5.7%] under full-day ell = 0 common-mode removal, mapping via L_c^vol/R_T to rho_T = 21.1 g/cm^3).

With rho_T = 20 g/cm^3 and a_eff held fixed and zero per-object refitting, three independent cross-scale tests follow. First, in 175 SPARC galaxies, an a priori Freeman-disk split separates 3-D rising-core onset scaling (alpha_rising = 0.256 +- 0.069 vs 1/3) from 2-D Keplerian tails (alpha_tail = 0.581 +- 0.086 vs 1/2; p = 0.0031), while the coupled-gradient force law reproduces the radial-acceleration (0.1330 dex RMS) and Baryonic Tully-Fisher (R^2 = 0.956, 0.0807 dex) relations. Second, four external R_earth-free astrophysical catalogs — outer-giant-planet spin (19.89 g/cm^3), the brown-dwarf rotational period floor (1.150 +- 0.075 hr vs TEP-saturated Chandrasekhar-Jacobi 1.155 hr [20.19 g/cm^3], sitting 8.3 sigma above the unscreened Newtonian Jacobi instability limit of 0.527 hr), compressed terrestrial moments of inertia (17.89 g/cm^3), and the Solar Model S convection-zone base (0.713 R_sun vs sqrt(3) R_T(M_sun) = 0.715 R_sun [20.15 g/cm^3]) — converge with GNSS to rho_T = 19.59 +- 1.10 g/cm^3 (19.68 +- 1.25 g/cm^3 GNSS-free; 20.05 +- 1.61 g/cm^3 v_gal-marginalized; look-elsewhere p = 2.9e-4 [3.6 sigma], 2.05% leave-one-channel-out blind prediction RMS). Third, the horizon crossover R_T(M_x) = R_S occurs at M_x ~ 3.04e7 M_sun, matching the runaway black hole candidate RBH-1.
