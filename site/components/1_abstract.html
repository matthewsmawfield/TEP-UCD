Abstract

Gravitational anomalies across astrophysical scales exhibit a dual scaling regularity: the radius at which Newtonian dynamics fails scales as $R_T(M) = (3M/4\pi\rho_T)^{1/3} \propto M^{1/3}$ in the compact saturation regime and $R_{\rm DM} \propto M^{1/2}$ in the low-acceleration galactic regime. Within the Temporal Equivalence Principle (TEP) two-branch scalar action ($P(X,\phi) = P_{\rm kin}(X) - \tfrac{1}{4}\lambda M_{\rm Pl}^4 u^4$, $A(u) = e^{-u}$), the quartic confining potential fixes the cubic screening response $S = (\bar\rho/\rho_T)^{1/3}$ around the saturation density $\rho_T \approx 20$ g/cm³ (calibrated from terrestrial GNSS and tested across astrophysical systems), while the nonlinear kinetic branch $P_{,X}(\xi) = k\sqrt{\xi} + 2\xi$ ($k = 16.03$) governs unscreened ($S \ll 1$) galactic halos at $a_{\rm eff} = 1.20 \times 10^{-10}$ m s$^{-2}$ (yielding under the potential-share identification $\epsilon_{\rm host} = a_\phi/(g_{\rm bar} + a_\phi)$ a parameter-free star-forming spiral BBH host tracking fraction of $\langle\epsilon_{\rm host}\rangle_{\rm SFR}^{\rm UCD} = 0.236 \pm 0.054$ at $R_{\rm eff}$ and $0.243 \pm 0.052$ across the radial Freeman disk [$0.197\text{--}0.202$ with $25\%$ bulge/NSC hosts], matching the GWTC-1–5.0 optimum in Paper 22). Across a 4-layer PREM Earth boundary-value problem and 1-D spherical nonlinear Picard solve, the transition-shell ($4{,}010$ km; $3{,}957$ km nonlinear) and full-volume ($3{,}213$ km; $3{,}170$ km nonlinear, $L_c^{\rm vol}/R_T = 0.775$) kernels reproduce both the 25.3-year GPS PPP clock correlation length ($L_c = 4{,}201$ km, $\hat\rho_T = 19.2$ g/cm³) and 2023 CDDIS RINEX3 single-constellation GPS, Galileo, and GLONASS reprocessing ($3{,}853$ km $\sqrt{N}$-weighted, and $3{,}155 \pm 181$ km [$\mathrm{CV} = 5.7\%$] under full-day $\ell = 0$ common-mode removal, mapping via $L_c^{\rm vol}/R_T$ to $\hat\rho_T = 21.1$ g/cm³).

With $\rho_T = 20$ g/cm³ and $a_{\rm eff}$ held fixed and zero per-object refitting, three independent cross-scale tests follow. First, in 175 SPARC galaxies, an a priori Freeman-disk split separates 3-D rising-core onset scaling ($\alpha_{\rm rising} = 0.256 \pm 0.069$ vs. $1/3$) from 2-D Keplerian tails ($\alpha_{\rm tail} = 0.581 \pm 0.086$ vs. $1/2$; $p = 0.0031$), while the coupled-gradient force law reproduces the radial-acceleration ($0.1330$ dex RMS) and Baryonic Tully–Fisher ($R^2 = 0.956, 0.0807$ dex) relations. Second, four external $R_\oplus$-free astrophysical catalogs — outer-giant-planet spin ($19.89$ g/cm³), the brown-dwarf rotational period floor ($1.150 \pm 0.075$ hr vs. TEP-saturated Chandrasekhar–Jacobi $1.155$ hr [$20.19$ g/cm³], sitting $8.3\sigma$ above the unscreened Newtonian Jacobi instability limit of $0.527$ hr), compressed terrestrial moments of inertia ($17.89$ g/cm³), and the Solar Model S convection-zone base ($0.713\,R_\odot$ vs. $\sqrt{3}R_T(M_\odot) = 0.715\,R_\odot$ [$20.15$ g/cm³]) — converge with GNSS to $\hat\rho_T = 19.59 \pm 1.10$ g/cm³ ($19.68 \pm 1.25$ g/cm³ GNSS-free; $20.05 \pm 1.61$ g/cm³ $v_{\rm gal}$-marginalized; look-elsewhere $p = 2.9 \times 10^{-4}$ [$3.6\sigma$], $2.05\%$ leave-one-channel-out blind prediction RMS). Third, the horizon crossover $R_T(M_\times) = R_S$ occurs at $M_\times \approx 3.04 \times 10^7\,M_\odot$, matching the runaway black hole candidate RBH-1.
